## About Me

I am **Hamza Khalid**, a **Systems Engineer** based in **Lahore, Pakistan**, with an **MS in Electrical Engineering** from the Information Technology University of the Punjab (ITU) and **7+ years** of industry experience across Pakistan and the Gulf.

My work runs on two tracks. On the **RF side** I build direction finding, spectrum monitoring and radar systems on **NI PXIe, FlexRIO, USRP, VSA and VST** platforms. On the **embedded side** I build **IIoT products** end to end: custom ESP32 and STM32 boards, industrial links over Modbus and MQTT, and the **cloud dashboards, desktop apps and AI** on top. For most of my career I was the **regional technical lead for National Instruments (NI) platforms** at an authorized NI distributor covering the UAE, Qatar, Kuwait, Oman and Pakistan.

- **RF Flagship:** 5-channel phase coherent MIMO direction finder with under 1 degree angle of arrival resolution (MS research)
- **Embedded Flagship:** Energy Monitoring System: ESP32 smart panel to AWS dashboard with LLM-based anomaly detection, built solo
- **Track Record:** Proposals, architecture and delivery for projects worth up to US$1M, as team lead or solo on every one
- **Professional Goal:** Build reliable systems that join RF and industrial hardware with data and AI

## 📡 Track 1: RF Systems

### Flagship Project: Multichannel Correlative Interferometer Direction Finding

<p align="center"><i>5-Channel Phase Coherent MIMO DF on NI PXIe and FlexRIO (MS Research)</i></p>

My flagship project is a **5-channel phase coherent MIMO direction finder** with a **5-element circular antenna array** covering **20 MHz to 8 GHz** in four bands. Five vector signal analyzers share one LO, FlexRIO FPGAs handle the processing, and **quadratic interpolation** improved angle of arrival resolution from the reference table's **2-5 degree steps to under 1 degree**.

**Key Components**

- **Three NI PXIe-1085 chassis** with five **PXIe-5668R VSAs** sharing one LO in star configuration
- **Six PXIe-7976R FlexRIO FPGAs** and a PXIe-8880 controller
- **Per-channel phase and amplitude calibration** with a PXIe-5840 VST and RF splitter
- **Runtime calibration health check** using a wideband noise source and circular baseline statistics
- **LabVIEW DSP chain:** phase coherent IQ, FFT, correlation against the antenna's 3D reference table, then quadratic curve fitting
- **Field tested** on FM broadcast, GSM-900 and a 2.4 GHz drone control link

Comparing magnitude-only, phase-only and full baseline correlation showed full baseline gave the most stable bearings.

## ⚙️ Track 2: Embedded, IIoT & Cloud

### Flagship Project: Energy Monitoring System with LLM Anomaly Detection

<p align="center"><i>Smart LV Panel to Cloud Dashboard, Built Solo</i></p>

My flagship product is an **end-to-end energy monitoring system** that I designed and built alone across **hardware, backend, cloud and AI**. A smart LV distribution panel streams three-phase power data through an **ESP32 IoT gateway** to a cloud backend, where dashboards, alerts and an **LLM-based anomaly detector** turn raw readings into decisions.

**Key Components**

- **Smart LV distribution panel** with an ESP32 IoT gateway
- **Three-phase metering:** voltage, current, power, power factor and kWh, plus temperature and humidity
- **MQTT/HTTPS** telemetry into a **Django + PostgreSQL** backend on **AWS EC2**
- **Dashboards, alerts and historical trends**
- **CSV, Excel and PDF reports**
- **LLM-based anomaly detection** on consumption patterns

It covers the full product stack I work across: electrical hardware, embedded firmware, cloud software and AI.
